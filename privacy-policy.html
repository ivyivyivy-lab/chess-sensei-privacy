Privacy Policy for Chess Sensei

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Chess Sensei app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles your information. Your privacy matters, and the App is built to respect it: Chess Sensei does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

Information We Collect

None. Chess Sensei does not collect any personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or any other personal detail. We have no servers and receive no data from the App.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves your game statistics (such as games played, results, and rating estimates) and your preferences directly on your device so the App can remember your progress between sessions. This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed if you delete the App.

No Network Connection

Chess Sensei works entirely offline. The chess engine and coaching features run locally on your device. The App does not connect to the internet, and therefore transmits no information to us or to any third party.

Third-Party Services, Analytics & Advertising

The App contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking of any kind. We do not use any software development kits that collect data about you or your device.

Children’s Privacy

Chess Sensei is suitable for users of all ages. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future — for example, if new features are added — we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
